Bonus points system

Live bonus points system (BPS) rankings per fixture, updated as each match is played — same as the official site's fixture page. The 3 highest-BPS players get 3 bonus points, the next 2, the next 1; tied players share the same bonus, and a tie "uses up" the slot(s) it occupies (e.g. two players tied for top get 3 each and nobody gets 2).

Players to consider buying

Ranked by recent form, FPL's own expected points for next gameweek, and points-per-cost among available (non-injured/suspended) players, adjusted for how easy their upcoming fixtures are.

My Team

Enter your FPL team ID to plan your next gameweek — a recommended starting XI and captain picked from your existing 15, a captaincy ranking, and transfer ideas — all while that gameweek's deadline is still open. Find your ID in the URL when viewing your team on the FPL site, e.g. fantasy.premierleague.com/entry/1234567/event/1.

Chip strategy

Timing suggestions for your unused chips, based on the blank/double gameweeks scheduled for the rest of the season. A rule of thumb, not a guarantee — always sanity-check against your actual squad's fixtures nearer the time.

Template comparison

The "template" here is the most-owned player at each position across all of FPL — not just the top 10k — built from each player's overall selected-by %.

Transfer suggestions

Each idea below is independent — it assumes you make just that one swap, starting fresh from your bank each time, not several transfers at once. They're numbered biggest-upgrade- first (#1 = the single best improvement available in your squad right now) by estimated points gained over your lookahead window — projected from recent form (or FPL's own expected points as a fallback) scaled by each upcoming fixture's difficulty. Replacements within each idea are also listed best-first. Always shows at least 5 (whenever your squad has that many valid options) so you can see the full picture — a green gain is worth making, a red one would cost you points and isn't recommended even with a free transfer to spend.

"Budget" is what you'd have to spend on a replacement if you sold that player alone: your bank + their current price — not necessarily your exact FPL sell price, which can be lower once a player has risen in value.

Squad Builder

Builds the best legal 15-man squad (2 GK / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 3 FWD, max 3 players from any one team) under a budget — for planning a Wildcard or Free Hit from scratch, rather than one transfer at a time. A Free Hit only lasts a single gameweek, so it's optimized for just that week; a Wildcard sticks around, so pick how many gameweeks ahead to weigh fixtures over. Loading a team on the My Team tab fills in its bank + squad value as the starting budget here — edit it freely, or use this without loading a team at all.

Mini-Leagues

Enter your FPL team ID to see the leagues you're in. Pick a league to view standings, then click a manager to see their transfer history, hits taken, chips used, and an estimated free transfer count (past-gameweek data is always public; the current gameweek's picks aren't until its deadline passes).
